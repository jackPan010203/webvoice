Refund Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

1. Monthly Subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time. If you cancel before your next billing date, you will not be charged again. We do not issue refunds for partial months already billed. To cancel, go to your Paddle account management page or contact us at support@webvoice.app.

2. Yearly Subscriptions

Yearly subscribers can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase if the service does not work as described. After 14 days, no refund is available, but you may cancel future renewals.

3. Lifetime Purchases

Lifetime License purchases are final. Due to the significant discount offered with the lifetime plan, all sales are final. No refunds are provided after purchase.

4. How to Request a Refund

Email support@webvoice.app with your purchase receipt (from Paddle) and reason for request. We process refund requests within 5–10 business days. Paddle will send the refund to your original payment method.

5. Chargebacks

We take payment disputes seriously. Before initiating a chargeback with your bank, please contact us first. Excessive chargebacks may result in account suspension.
