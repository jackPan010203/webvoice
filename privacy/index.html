Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

WebVoice ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

  • Local processing: Text you select is processed entirely on your device. We never receive or store this content.
  • Cloud AI requests: When you use cloud AI voices, the text is temporarily sent to our provider (MiniMax) to generate audio. It is not stored after playback completes.
  • Usage data: We collect anonymized metrics (characters read, features used) to improve our service. This data cannot identify you.
  • Account data: If you purchase a subscription, we store your email and order history. Payment processing is handled by Paddle, Inc., a PCI-DSS compliant provider.

2. How We Use Data

  • To deliver cloud TTS audio to you
  • To manage your subscription
  • To improve WebVoice performance and features
  • To respond to support requests

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with:

  • MiniMax (TTS processing provider)
  • Paddle (payment processing — you can find their privacy policy at paddle.com/legal/privacy)
  • Cloud providers (hosting, analytics) under strict confidentiality

4. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for subscription management only. No advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at privacy@webvoice.app.

6. Data Retention

  • Local content: Never stored by us
  • Cloud TTS: Deleted immediately after playback
  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account

7. Children's Privacy

WebVoice is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes via the extension or email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Email: privacy@webvoice.app
