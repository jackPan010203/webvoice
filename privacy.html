Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

WebVoice collects minimal data to operate the Cloud TTS service. When you use Cloud TTS, the text you input is sent to our servers for processing. We do not store your text after audio generation is complete.

2. Local Reading Mode

Local Reading (the free tier) uses only your device's built-in speech synthesis engine. No data is transmitted to our servers. We do not collect any data when Local Reading is used.

3. Payment Data

All payment processing is handled by Paddle. We do not collect or store your credit card or payment details. Please review Paddle's privacy policy for how they handle your payment data.

4. What We Store

We store: your email address (for account & subscription management), subscription status, and basic usage statistics (e.g., API call counts for billing purposes). We do not store the text content you process.

5. Cookies

WebVoice website uses minimal essential cookies for basic functionality. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Data Retention

Subscription records are retained for as long as your account is active. Upon cancellation, your data is retained for 30 days for legal and billing purposes, then deleted.

7. Third Parties

We use Paddle for payment processing and Google Firebase for basic analytics. Any third-party services used are bound by their respective privacy policies.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@webvoice.app. We respond to all legitimate requests within 30 days.

9. Data Security

We implement standard encryption (TLS) for all data in transit. We do not guarantee absolute security but take commercially reasonable measures to protect your data.

10. Contact

Questions? Contact us at support@webvoice.app
