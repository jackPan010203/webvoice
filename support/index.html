Get help with WebVoice

How do I install WebVoice?

Search "WebVoice" in the Chrome Web Store and click "Add to Chrome". No account needed.

Why isn't the extension working?

Make sure the extension is enabled in chrome://extensions. Try refreshing the page. If it still doesn't work, disable and re-enable the extension.

How do I upgrade to Pro?

Click the WebVoice icon in your Chrome toolbar, go to Settings, and choose a plan. You'll be redirected to our secure payment page.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — Pro and Team subscribers can request a full refund within 7 days of purchase. Email support@webvoice.app with your order details.

Does local reading use my internet?

Local voices are generated entirely on your device and do not require internet. Cloud AI voices do require a connection.

Still need help?

Email us at support@webvoice.app and we'll get back to you within 1–2 business days.
